Movie Summary

Interstellar is a science-fiction movie about a group of astronauts who travel through a wormhole near Saturn in search of a new home for humanity. The story explores space, time, love, sacrifice, and the relationship between a father and his daughter.

Actors

Matthew McConaughey as Cooper, a former NASA pilot who becomes the leader of the space mission.

Anne Hathaway as Dr. Amelia Brand, a scientist and astronaut who joins Cooper on the mission.

Jessica Chastain as Murphy Cooper, Cooper's daughter who grows up to become a brilliant scientist.

Favorite Quote

"Love is the one thing we're capable of perceiving that transcends dimensions of time and space."
